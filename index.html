Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How long does an epoxy floor last?

With proper care, quality epoxy floors last many years and resist stains, water and abrasion.

How long does installation take?

Most residential floors take 2–4 days including curing; larger projects vary.

Can epoxy be applied over existing floors?

Usually yes, after inspection and surface preparation.

Do you make custom resin tables?

Yes — choose the wood, resin colour and size, and we craft it to order.
